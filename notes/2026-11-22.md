# 2026-11-22 — Kinetic type: display words must fit the card, and subtitles must clear the strip

Built the Sam Altman "13 rules" kinetic-type edit (20.5 s, 720x1280) and the layout exposed three bugs: display words bled off the square card edges even at the minimum font size, speaker display words sat over the face on close-ups, and subtitles collided with the torn-paper strip.

Lesson: in kinetic type, guarantee every display word fits inside its card (now capped at 640 px wide), drop the display word on close-up speaker beats so the face stays clear, and lift subtitles above any bottom strip — decoration must never fight the text it frames.
