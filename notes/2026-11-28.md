# 2026-11-28 — Fixing the 3D world's trees, river and waterfall

The graphics upgrade feedback was specific: the trees looked fake, the river was missing, and the waterfall was bad. Rebuilt the trees with bark, branches and real leaf texture, found the river bug — the water plane was built upside down, so it was invisible from above — and rebuilt the waterfall as two curved sheets over rock with foam and mist.

Lesson: when something is missing in 3D, check the orientation before rebuilding the geometry.
