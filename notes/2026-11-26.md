# 2026-11-26 — Build the 3D world from ready-made assets

Started building the explorable 3D world (library, garden, forest, river) as a browser artifact. The lesson from the night: don't build graphics from zero — use ready-made free 3D assets (e.g. Meshy.ai) and spend the effort on lighting, camera movement and making it feel like a real place, because a from-scratch world ends up white and laggy.
