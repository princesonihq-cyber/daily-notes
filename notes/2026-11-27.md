# 2026-11-27 — Realistic graphics upgrade for the 3D world

After the first 3D world went live, the feedback was clear: it needs to feel real, not stylised. Started the graphics upgrade — realistic lighting, shadows and richer materials for the library, garden, forest and river, using ready-made assets instead of building from zero.

Lesson: a world only feels real when light and texture do the work, not just geometry.
