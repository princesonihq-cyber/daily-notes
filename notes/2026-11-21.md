# 2026-11-21 — Google indexing quota is a daily budget, spend it on the best pages

Requested indexing for the six remaining princearon.us.ci pages on Nov 17 and hit "Quota Exceeded" — Google only allows a limited number of indexing requests per day per property, so the requests failed and the pages stayed unindexed.

Lesson: treat indexing requests like a budget — submit the highest-value pages first, space the rest across days, and let Google discover the others naturally through the sitemap and internal links instead of burning the quota all at once.
